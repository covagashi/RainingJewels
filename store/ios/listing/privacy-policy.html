Privacy Policy

Raining Jewels · Last updated: 2 August 2026

Raining Jewels (“the App”) is a free ambient-sound player. This policy explains what data the App handles and what it does not.

Summary

We do not collect personal data. The App does not require an account, does not show ads, does not track you, and does not need a network connection to play sounds.

Data stored on your device only

The App may save the following preferences locally on your device:

  • Whether you have dismissed the welcome screen
  • The last sound you played
  • Your volume preference

These values never leave your device. They are not uploaded to us or to any third party. Clearing the App’s data or uninstalling the App removes them.

What we do not collect

  • Name, email, or any contact information
  • Location
  • Device identifiers for advertising or analytics
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to us
  • Payment or purchase data (the App is free and has no in-app purchases)
  • Microphone, camera, contacts, photos, or similar sensors/data

Network

All ambient sounds ship inside the App binary. Playback works fully offline. The App does not contact a backend service to stream audio or to operate.

Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your region).

Third parties

The App does not embed advertising, analytics, or social SDKs that would share data with third parties.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
